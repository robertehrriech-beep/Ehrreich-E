About 3B Consulting

Build. Business. Body.

3B Consulting is a division of Ehrreich Enterprises, founded to provide holistic executive development and organizational leadership solutions. Our mission is to cultivate resilient leaders and high-performing teams by integrating operational expertise, strategic insight, and physical resilience. We believe that true leadership is built on a foundation of mental, emotional, and physical strength, and we are committed to guiding our clients on a transformative journey that enhances their personal and professional growth.

Your organization deserves leaders who are built to last. We partner with you to forge structural vision, sharp corporate acumen, and lasting physical resilience. Meet the team ready to build the next generation of leadership with you.

Our Story

Founded in January 2025, 3B Consulting was established to bridge the gap between traditional corporate management and holistic executive development. Over the past year, we have experienced rapid growth by partnering with organizations that recognize a simple truth: resilient businesses are built by resilient leaders. By focusing on the complete individual, we cultivate leadership capacity that withstands modern disruption.

Meet The Leadership Team

The expertise behind our transformative approach.

Bob Ehrreich

Owner & CEO

Bob's leadership philosophy is forged from a 23-year military career as a Senior Noncommissioned Officer and Ranger-qualified infantryman, including 15 years of enterprise level operational leadership from the fire team to the Pentagon. A dedicated strength athlete and endurance runner, Bob champions the "Body" pillar of the firm, utilizing sports psychology and progressive overload to teach executives how to build mental toughness and operational capacity.

Coleen Crawford

President of Administration & HR

Coleen drives the structural and human development core of 3B Consulting. Holding a Master's degree in Psychology, she provides deep insights into organizational behavior, team dynamics, and workplace culture. Her academic expertise is matched by her background in elite fitness coaching; as a Certified Personal Trainer and certified CrossFit Coach, Coleen designs our programs to combat executive burnout, ensuring our clients achieve sustainable, high-performance results.
