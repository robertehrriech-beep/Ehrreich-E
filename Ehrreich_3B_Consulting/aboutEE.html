About Ehrreich Enterprises

A Family-Run Legacy of Service, Development, and Care.

Our Vision for the Future

Ehrreich Enterprises is a family-run organization founded on a clear, enduring conviction: lasting prosperity is measured by the strength of the communities we uplift and the legacy we leave behind. We envision a stronger future for our community and the betterment of the world—driven not by short-term gains, but by principled leadership, purposeful growth, and genuine stewardship.

Rooted in decades of disciplined operational leadership and family values, Ehrreich Enterprises serves as the strategic home for ventures that strengthen individuals, modernize organizations, build resilient spaces, and stand beside those who serve.

Our Three Foundational Pillars

Service

Service is the foundation of everything we build. We lead from the front with humility and accountability, dedicating our time, resources, and strategic reach to strengthen our local communities and support missions that create meaningful, measurable impact.

Development

We believe in building capacity that endures. Whether developing resilient executive leaders through 3B Consulting or planning long-term community infrastructure through Reich Commercial Real Estate, we invest in disciplined growth that equips people and places for tomorrow.

Care

True leadership never loses sight of the human element. Through genuine care for the well-being of our neighbors, partners, and veterans—embodied in our vision for 12 Warriors Strong Nonprofit—we ensure that every venture prioritizes dignity, wellness, and long-term human flourishing.
