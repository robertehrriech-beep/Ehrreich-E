About 3B Consulting

Build. Business. Body. We believe true leadership requires structural vision, corporate acumen, and physical resilience. Discover the team driving the next generation of leadership.

Our Story

Founded in January 2025, 3B Consulting was established to bridge the gap between traditional corporate management and holistic executive development. Over the past year, we have experienced rapid growth by partnering with organizations that recognize a simple truth: resilient businesses are built by resilient leaders. By focusing on the complete individual, we cultivate leadership capacity that withstands modern disruption.

Meet The Leadership Team

The expertise behind our transformative approach.

Bob Ehrreich

Owner & CEO

Bob's leadership philosophy is forged from a blend of advanced management strategy and practical, hands-on experience coordinating operations and personnel within the military. He brings a disciplined, mission-focused mindset to corporate consulting. A dedicated strength athlete, Bob champions the "Body" pillar of the firm, utilizing principles of progressive overload and physical endurance to teach executives how to build mental toughness and operational capacity.

Coleen Crawford

President of Administration & HR

Coleen drives the structural and human development core of 3B Consulting. Holding a Master's degree in Sociology, she provides deep insights into organizational behavior, team dynamics, and workplace culture. Her academic expertise is matched by her background in elite fitness coaching; as a Certified Personal Trainer and certified CrossFit Coach, Coleen designs our programs to combat executive burnout, ensuring our clients achieve sustainable, high-performance results.
